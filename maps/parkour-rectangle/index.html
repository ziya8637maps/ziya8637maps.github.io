The map features a variety of thematic environments, including deserts, birch forests, skyblock jumps, and more. These diverse settings offer both visual appeal and a range of parkour puzzles to tackle, making Parkour Rectangle an exciting and enjoyable adventure for parkour enthusiasts of all skill levels.
